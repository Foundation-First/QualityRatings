What CLASS measures

The Classroom Assessment Scoring System (CLASS) rates teacher-child interactions on a 1–7 scale: 1–2 is low, 3–5 is mid-range and 6–7 is high. VQB5 uses the Infant, Toddler and Pre-K versions, and every birth-to-five classroom is observed twice a year.

Local observations

Scheduled through Ready Regions and conducted by locally identified, reliable observers. Every classroom gets two a year, one in fall and one in spring, with in-person feedback to the teacher within 10 days. Used for classroom-level information and coaching.

External observations

Coordinated for the state by Teachstone observers. Only some classrooms are observed, but every site gets at least one per age level, with an automated report to the site administrator. Used to check accuracy and consistency across Ready Regions.
