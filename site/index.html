Where sites land

VQB5 combines classroom interaction scores (CLASS) and curriculum use into a total score from 100 to 800. Each site earns a rating level (Needs Support 100–399, Meets Expectations 400–699, Exceeds Expectations 700–800). Because Meets Expectations covers such a wide span, the 100-point ranges show where within it sites fall.
