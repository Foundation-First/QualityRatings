Honor Rolls

Excellence Honor Roll = total score of 700 or higher (Exceeds Expectations). Improvement Honor Roll = top 10% for interaction-point gains among returning sites. Infant & Toddler Honor Roll = top 10% for infant/toddler interaction scores. A site can receive more than one Honor Roll each year.

Percentages may not sum to 100 due to rounding. Regional counts are computed from VDOE's published site-level data and match VDOE and regional summaries.
