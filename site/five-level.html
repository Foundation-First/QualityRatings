What is changing with the five-level ratings?

Beginning with the 2026-27 program year, VQB5 shifts from three rating levels to five, in 100-point steps. The new ratings will be issued in fall 2027.

Level descriptions are from VDOE's Fall 2026 Data Deep Dive.

How sites would be rated

Modeled with 2025-26 scores. The current three-level rating is on the left; the same scores placed in the five new levels are on the right.

What else is changing

  • Curriculum points will be awarded only to sites that choose to use an approved curriculum in every classroom, beginning with the 2026-27 program year.
  • Starting in fall 2027, sites rated Needs Support or Approaching Expectations (499 points and lower) will take part in VDOE site improvement planning.
